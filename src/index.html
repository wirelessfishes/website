My thoughts on generative AI

It is a tool. It is not a replacement for human creativity.

It should be utilized to automate mundane tasks to allow humans to focus on what they truly enjoy.

This might come across as weird, considering I am a member of the no ai webring. It is up to everyone to draw their line in the sand. Mine is right next to actual "content" (text, images, videos, ...) and straight up using AI to generate full programs/websites.

I have utilized AI in my backend extensively exactly because of this. I have no interest in battling with sqlite and expressjs and whatever else. I want a functioning guestbook and an api. That means I will frequently prompt AI with questions about libraries, proper practises, common pitfalls, debug errors, etc... as this is much faster than staring at a console output for 2 hours, browsing reddit for 4 hours and the documentation for another hour.

Even though gen-AI is utilized in development of this site, not a single character of text content or a single pixel in the images on my site is generated by artificial intelligence. And it will stay that way until the end of time.

- Sharky, 2026
